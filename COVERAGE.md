# Content Coverage & Question-Bank Notes

## V2 bank size
3,200 original practice-question instances.

## Important
This project does **not** contain leaked, copied, or pirated SHRM proprietary questions.
It is designed to approximate SHRM-CP decision patterns using original practice content.

## Official structure used
- 134 total multiple-choice questions
- 80 stand-alone knowledge items
- 54 situational-judgment items
- 220 minutes of testing time
- Two 110-minute sections
- SHRM BASK is the exam blueprint
- SHRM's official prep product advertises 2,700+ practice questions

## Answer-pattern protection
The stored bank is evenly balanced across A/B/C/D.
More importantly, each test session re-maps answer positions again.
The engine prevents the same correct letter from occurring more than twice consecutively.

## Content-development target
The bank architecture is intentionally larger than SHRM's advertised 2,700+ practice-question pool.
Future content revisions should prioritize breadth and item quality rather than simply increasing count.
